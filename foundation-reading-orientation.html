FOUNDATION · BEFORE YOU READ A WORD

Orient yourself on a Jewish page before you translate it.

Meaning comes second; orientation comes first. This unit trains the moves every reader of the canon makes before reading: find the primary text and tell it from the talk around it, spot who is speaking, mark where one idea stops and the next starts, and hear when a source is asking rather than telling. Then it meets the small signal words that carry all of it — question particles, connectors, and the quotation and attribution formulas — on real lines of Gemara, Mishnah, Avot, and Torah.

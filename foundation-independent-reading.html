FOUNDATION · THE INDEPENDENT LEARNER’S MOVES

Drive your own reading, with no teacher beside you.

Orientation gets you onto the page; these moves let you stay there alone. This unit trains the habits of an independent learner: see where a translation has already chosen for you, restate a case in your own words, give each side of a dispute its strongest form, and tell a literacy question from one that belongs with a person — then predict before you check, ask an answerable question, reach for the right tool, and choose a next move you can defend, on real sources from the Akedah to Eruvin to the Amidah.

INDEPENDENT-READING TRAIL

    Translation, restatement, steelman, and knowing when to ask — then the agency to question, choose a tool, and pick what comes next.

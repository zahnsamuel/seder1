CHUMASH ARC · A THIRD GENRE

Read the line that says it twice.

Law taught you reception; the Akeidah taught you narrative. Poetry thinks differently — in paired half-lines that echo, sharpen, or oppose each other, in metaphors that argue, and in grammar that turns. One psalm’s deepest move is a single pronoun.

POETRY READING TRAIL

    Parallelism first — echoing, advancing, opposing — then metaphor, then the pronoun turn, then a psalm’s afterlife in daily prayer.

Meet six of the thirteen middot.

Every day's prayers open with a baraita naming thirteen principles through which the Torah is expounded. This unit introduces the six most commonly used: kal vachomer, gezeirah shavah, binyan av, and the three klal/prat patterns — the reasoning tools behind many Gemara arguments you will read.

MIDDOT SOURCE TRAIL

    A middah is not a translation exercise. It is a rule for how one verse's law can extend — or not extend — to another case.

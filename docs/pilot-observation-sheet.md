# Seder learner pilot observation sheet

For each learner, observe one placement, one guided source unit, one retrieval session, one source-map activity, and one independent checkpoint.

Record only product evidence:

- Did the learner know what to do next without prompting?
- Could they locate the relevant line on the source?
- Did they use translation as support or as an escape from reading?
- Could they explain why an answer was right or wrong?
- Did the recommendation feel relevant after a miss?
- Which terms, instructions, or visual elements caused friction?
- Did the learner leave with greater confidence in approaching a text?

Do not use pilot observations as claims of educational efficacy without a defined study design and participant consent process.

# Jewish Learning Academy Pilot Survey

Give this short survey immediately after the session. Use a 1–5 scale for scored questions:
1 = strongly disagree, 5 = strongly agree.

## Confidence and clarity

1. I understood what Jewish Learning Academy was helping me become able to do.
2. I knew what to do next without needing someone to explain the screen.
3. The session gave me one manageable learning task rather than too many choices.
4. I felt respected regardless of how much Jewish education I had before today.
5. I can name a Jewish-learning skill I practiced today.
6. I feel more able to open a Jewish source than I did before the session.
7. The time commitment felt sustainable for an ordinary week.
8. I would choose to return for another session.

## Short answers

9. Complete the sentence: “After today, I can…”
10. What was the most confusing moment?
11. What felt most like real Jewish learning?
12. What would you want Jewish Learning Academy to help you learn next?
13. Was there a word, source, or assumption that made you feel left out or behind?
14. If you would not return, what is the main reason?

## Optional background

Do not require denominational identity or detailed personal history. Ask only what is needed to
interpret the pilot:

- Before today, how comfortable were you opening a Jewish text? (1–5)
- Which settings, if any, have shaped your Jewish learning?
- Which source families are you most curious about?

# Seder private-pilot protocol

Invite 5–12 motivated English-speaking learners who can sound out Hebrew but do not yet read Gemara independently.

Ask each learner to complete placement, one Berakhot session, one review session, and one tractate lab. Observe rather than teach through the interface. Capture only product observations: where the learner hesitates, whether the source and translation are understandable, whether the next action is evident, and whether the learner can explain the Gemara move in their own words.

Success is not high XP. Success is a learner returning to the Daf, locating the question or answer, and knowing what to try next without a study partner. Do not use pilot data for public claims until the pilot design and privacy notice are finalized.

Copy-paste templates to line up ~5–15 real learners for the private pilot (get signal before the
Aug 18 demo). **Audience:** independent adults ~18–35 entering the Jewish canon for the first time —
no assumed Hebrew, day-school, or study-partner background; "invitation, not insider status."
**Link:** https://seder-demo.onrender.com (name-only signup, no password/email, free).

## Sending tips

- The **personal DM (#1)** converts best — send it to 15–20 people individually rather than one blast;
  aim to land 5–15 who actually start.
- **Personalize the first line** ("I thought of you because you mentioned wanting to learn Gemara / read
  the siddur…") — it roughly doubles reply rates.
- Ask them to start **the day you send it** — momentum matters for getting signal before the 18th.
- After a few days, open `analytics.html` (with your admin token) to see who's active and what feedback
  came in — that's your live pilot signal for the demo's honesty pivot.

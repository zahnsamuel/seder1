## Cognitive-load checks

- Did the learner ever ask, “What am I supposed to do?”
- Did more than one action appear equally primary?
- What language, icon, or concept required explanation?
- What did the learner ignore?
- Where did the learner hesitate for more than ten seconds?
- Did the learner understand why this skill came next?

## Jewish-learning dignity checks

- Did any copy make the learner feel tested, behind, or judged?
- Did the learner encounter an unfamiliar Hebrew term without usable support?
- Did the source feel like real Jewish learning rather than trivia?
- Did the learner see more than Gemara as part of Jewish literacy?
- Did a practical-halakhic or sensitive topic include a responsible-learning boundary?

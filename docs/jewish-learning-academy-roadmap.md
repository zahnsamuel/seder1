# Jewish Learning Academy Roadmap

## Product Thesis

Jewish Learning Academy helps a learner go from 0 to 1 in Jewish learning: from insecurity and fragmentation to the confidence to open the Jewish canon, recognize what kind of text they are seeing, and know how to keep learning.

The product is skills-first, not content-first. Gemara remains a powerful laboratory, but the goal is broader: Jewish literacy across the canon.

## Learner Promise

A learner who completes the Foundation path should be able to:

- orient inside Torah, Mishnah, Gemara, Halakha, Tanakh, Midrash, history, and Jewish thought
- recognize common source forms and learning moves
- follow a guided Jewish text session without feeling lost
- build daily rhythm through one clear next step at a time
- graduate with a visible capability profile and a path forward

## Design Principles

- One thing at a time
- Low cognitive load
- Opinionated sequencing
- Skill graph before content browsing
- Daily rhythm over heroic bursts
- Guided checks over required free typing
- Sources as laboratories for durable skills
- Human dignity for insecure beginners

## Current Strengths

- Foundation skill graph is active
- Daily sessions now route through Academy rhythm
- Placement captures learner level and schedule
- My Path shows capability progress
- Source links are being verified
- Full automated suite is passing in recent runs
- Preflight now checks core learner and trust surfaces

## What Still Needs Work

- Supabase hosted persistence and account isolation remain the real pilot blocker
- The skill graph needs to grow from dozens of skills toward a few hundred
- More non-Gemara canon pathways should be first-class
- Learner analytics should show where people stall or recover
- Human QA is needed on privacy, terms, support, and sensitive learning boundaries
- Pilot feedback should drive the next simplification pass

## Next Build Priorities

1. Finish the public JLA identity across docs, titles, and learner-facing copy.
2. Expand the foundation skill graph into a few hundred interlocking skills.
3. Convert more content units into explicit skill lessons.
4. Make daily sessions feel even more like a 20-minute Academy lesson.
5. Add learner-facing graduation levels and capability badges.
6. Build mentor/instructor review views for pilot feedback.
7. Complete Supabase isolation verification before real pilot users.
8. Run a human pilot and let real learner confusion reshape the product.

## North Star

Become someone who can learn the Jewish canon.

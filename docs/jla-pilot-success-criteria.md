# Jewish Learning Academy Pilot Success Criteria

The first pilot tests the 0-to-1 learner promise, not broad content completion or advanced
mastery. Review the evidence after every five learners; change one important thing at a time.

## Non-negotiable launch gates

- Hosted persistence is active.
- Two-account isolation verification passes.
- No learner sees another learner’s profile, evidence, notes, or activity.
- Every pilot source citation and translation has completed the review register.
- Privacy, terms, support, and sensitive-learning boundaries receive a human read.
- There are zero unresolved blocker defects on placement, Today, Academy session, or sign-out.

## Pilot outcome thresholds

| Outcome | Initial success threshold | Evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Understand the product promise | At least 80% can explain it without founder language | Observation + survey |
| Find the next action | At least 80% begin the assigned session without intervention | Observation |
| Complete a focused session | At least 75% complete one source-and-check cycle | Event log + observation |
| Name a capability | At least 70% complete “After today, I can…” with a real learning move | Survey |
| Confidence gain | Median post-session confidence is at least 1 point above baseline | Survey |
| Sustainable rhythm | At least 70% rate the chosen rhythm 4 or 5 | Survey |
| Return intent | At least 70% rate return intent 4 or 5 | Survey |
| Canon breadth | Every cohort encounters at least three non-Gemara source families | Event log |
| Dignity | Fewer than 10% report feeling judged or left behind | Survey + observation |
| Reliability | At least 95% of started sessions finish without a technical error | Event log |

## Product-learning questions

- Does placement choose a useful starting capability rather than merely a plausible level?
- Does “one thing at a time” survive real use, including errors and missed days?
- Do learners understand that Gemara is one powerful laboratory inside a wider Jewish canon?
- Which capability statements actually increase confidence?
- Where does Hebrew support help, and where does it become clutter?
- Which post-Foundation pathway do learners choose when no founder is present?

## Stop and repair conditions

Pause recruitment and repair before adding learners if any of these occur:

- Any account-isolation or privacy failure.
- A source, translation, or practical-halakhic boundary is materially wrong.
- Two learners in a row cannot identify the primary action on the same screen.
- A learner reports that the experience mocks, shames, or excludes their background.
- More than 20% of sessions fail for the same technical reason.

## Decision rule after the first cohort

Proceed to a larger pilot only if every non-negotiable gate is green and at least seven of the
ten outcome thresholds are met. Otherwise, rank observed failures by learner harm and frequency,
ship the smallest coherent repair, and repeat with a small cohort.

# The Jewish Learning Academy

**Become someone who can open Jewish texts.** A skills-first tutor that takes an adult from zero to
reading the Jewish canon on their own — one move at a time.

---

### The problem
Millions of Jewishly-curious adults feel locked out of the canon — no yeshiva, no day school, unsure
how to even open a page. Every existing tool hands them *content* (translations, courses, references).
None of them make a person *able to read on their own.*

### The bet
Teach the **transferable reading skills**, not the content. Modeled on Math Academy's knowledge graph:
one skill at a time, minimal cognitive load, always the single highest-leverage next move. The canon
becomes the practice ground; the product is literacy itself — "I can open a Jewish text and make sense
of it myself."

### What's built — and live today
A working end-to-end product ([seder-demo.onrender.com](https://seder-demo.onrender.com), 10-second
name-only signup):

- **Adaptive placement** that pins a learner's frontier in ~7–15 self-checks (not one per skill) by
  inferring everything below what they can do.
- A **55-skill knowledge graph** of reading moves, from recognizing Hebrew letters up to reading an
  unvocalized source independently — every skill knowing its prerequisites.
- **Scaffolded lessons** on real sources: introduce → practice → **transfer to a different genre**. The
  transfer *is* the skill.
- **Spaced review, targeted remediation, and a Hebrew-decoding on-ramp** — the full 0→1 arc, from the
  alphabet to independent reading.

### Where it honestly stands
This is a **v0.1 prototype with a real engine.** The learning machinery is built and working. What it
needs next — and we are deliberately not faking — is **(1)** a live pilot to calibrate difficulty and
validate the graph *(running now)*, and **(2)** educator sign-off and authored assessment banks
*(the tooling to capture both is built)*. Growth of the graph is gated on real pilot signal, not
speculation.

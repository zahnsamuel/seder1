# Academy Foundation Graduation Contract

## Purpose

Foundation graduation marks a meaningful 0→1 transition. It does not claim that a learner has mastered Jewish learning, completed a tractate, or received practical halakhic authority.

## Required evidence

A learner graduates after demonstrating:

1. source orientation on at least three source forms;
2. recurring Hebrew or translation signal recognition;
3. source-role and question/answer distinction;
4. case mapping with actors, action, and uncertainty;
5. claim/evidence/response tracking;
6. comparison of two sources with scope awareness;
7. a context move involving time, place, or community;
8. an independent first pass through an unfamiliar source;
9. a source-grounded question and evidence pointer;
10. a justified next learning move.

Evidence must include at least three different source forms and one unseen source. A correct answer in one familiar context is not enough.

## Learner-facing result

The graduate receives a capability profile, not a rank:

- “You can now open and orient yourself in a Jewish source.”
- “You can track a basic case, question, and response.”
- “You can compare two sources without flattening their differences.”
- “You can make a supported first pass through an unfamiliar source.”
- “Your next academy is ready when you are.”

## Boundaries

- Graduation is literacy evidence, not practical guidance.
- Learners may continue in the Academy without completing every optional skill.
- A learner can revisit any skill; graduation is not a finish screen.
- Human review may override an automated recommendation when evidence is ambiguous.

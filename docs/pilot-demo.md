# Seder pilot demonstration

## The claim

Seder gives independent beginners a serious path into Jewish textual literacy: source-first learning, real mastery evidence, spaced retrieval, adaptive repair, and one integrated canon rather than disconnected subjects.

## Five-minute walkthrough

1. Open the 30-Day Journey and show a learner's daily path.
2. Complete a Pilot Foundations source; deliberately miss once and open the targeted repair.
3. Show an Anchor Unit with Hebrew glosses, translation, notebook link, and full-source link.
4. Open the Learner Dashboard to show evidence, accuracy, and the next recommendation.
5. Open the Mastery Map / Daf Workbench to show the progression from beginner support to real source work.

## What Seder measures

- correct and incorrect source moves
- distinct source contexts for transfer
- retrieval and review needs
- pilot-path attendance, repair attempts, and voluntary feedback

## What Seder does not claim

It is a study aid, not a posek or substitute for qualified guidance. Every learner-facing source requires editorial verification before external pilot use.

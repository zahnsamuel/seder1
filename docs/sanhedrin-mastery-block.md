# Sanhedrin mastery block

**Opening source:** Mishnah Sanhedrin 1:1; Sanhedrin 2a.

## Learner outcomes

1. Map a court category, its case type, and its stated number.
2. Distinguish a category label from the cases that specify it.
3. Follow a Gemara question about why a Mishnah repeats or specifies language.
4. Explain an institutional reason without treating study as judicial authority.
5. Transfer the map to an unfamiliar source about procedure or testimony.

## Required sequence

Orientation → court/category map → close-reading question → competing explanation → institutional purpose → authority boundary → retrieval → unfamiliar-source transfer → typed capstone.

## Safety boundary

Seder teaches the textual structure of courts and legal reasoning. It does not adjudicate disputes, interpret law for a live case, or exercise judicial authority.

## Review packet

Before release, verify the exact source text and translation, the explanation of “what are these” as a reading proposal, every assessment answer, and the institutional-purpose framing against the source-review schema.

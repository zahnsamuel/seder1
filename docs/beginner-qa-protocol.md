# Seder beginner QA protocol

Test every new course as if you have never learned Jewish text before.

For each session, ask:

1. Can I tell what text I am reading and why it matters before I answer?
2. Is the Hebrew visible, and is translation help close at hand without taking over?
3. Does the question test one precise move rather than general confidence?
4. If I miss, do I understand why and know the next repair action?
5. Can I find the next step without scrolling or guessing?
6. After a short sequence, can I explain what changed in my understanding?

Log every confusing screen as: page, source, task, observed friction, likely cause, and proposed learning fix. Prioritize friction that blocks a learner from reaching the source itself.

# Seder Foundation Year

## Learner outcome

After 120 source-based sessions, a beginner can enter a short Mishnah or opening sugya with guided English support; identify case, question, proof, distinction, and response; retrieve core Hebrew/Aramaic signals; and explain why a wider-canon source belongs beside the Gemara move. This is a foundation for independent study, not certification or practical halakhic authority.

## Learning rhythm

Each ten-session block contains six new source encounters, two language/retrieval encounters, one contrasting-source transfer, and one integrated Canon Connection. Every session has one visible next move and all practice checks are shuffled choices; private notes are optional.

| Block | Gemara spine | Wider-canon integration | Demonstrated habit |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Berakhot 2a–2b | Shema, prayer, Torah language | Question, context, cited verse |
| 2 | Shabbat 2a and 9b | Prayer, time, responsibility | Case map, domain, stated concern |
| 3 | Eruvin 2a | Community, boundary, history | Measure, condition, formulation |
| 4 | Pesachim 2a | Freedom, ritual memory, Chumash | Word question, evidence chain, purpose |
| 5 | Bava Metzia 2a | Ethics, property, Mussar | Competing claims, procedure, fairness |
| 6 | Consolidation I | Tefillah and Halakha | Retrieval across source forms |
| 7 | Sukkah 2a | Embodied memory, Chassidus | Multiple reasons and distinction |
| 8 | Bava Kamma 2a | Restitution, Torah law | Category, difference, shared principle |
| 9 | Berakhot/Shabbat return | History and Jewish Thought | Compare parallel source moves |
| 10 | Eruvin/Pesachim return | Wider-world encounter | Context before comparison |
| 11 | Bava Metzia/Sukkah return | Mussar and Chassidus | Transfer to an unfamiliar source |
| 12 | Integrated capstone | All domains | Navigate, retrieve, connect, repair |

## Production standard

No block is complete until every session has a verified primary source, readable Hebrew, contextual English, shuffled diagnostic choices, explanation feedback, delayed retrieval, a source-specific transfer, and a responsible-learning boundary whenever practice, law, grief, or pastoral questions are nearby.

## Review and pilot gates

1. Editorial reviewer clears source, translation, context, assessment, accessibility, and safety gates.
2. Internal learner-flow QA reaches the block's next Gemara move without a stalled Continue action.
3. Small independent-learner pilot measures completion, misses, return rate, and one-week retrieval before the next block is expanded.

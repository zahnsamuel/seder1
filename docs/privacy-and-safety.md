# Learner privacy and safety baseline

Seder should collect only what it needs to operate a learner’s mastery path: account email, display name, and learning records such as attempts, mastery, XP, and reviews. Learner records are private and protected by Supabase row-level security.

Before a public launch, publish a plain-language privacy notice covering data purpose, retention, deletion/export requests, support contact, and any analytics. If Seder serves minors, obtain legal review of the applicable consent, parental-notice, and education-privacy requirements before collecting their personal data.

Seder is a learning platform. It should label instructional material as study support and should not present itself as a source of halakhic rulings or personal religious direction.

# Accessibility and mobile baseline

- Every interactive source line and answer must be reachable and usable by keyboard.
- Focus states must remain visible after an answer is submitted and when the Continue control becomes available.
- Hebrew text needs `lang="he"` and `dir="rtl"`; English explanations remain left-to-right.
- Do not rely on color alone for mastery, correctness, or unlock state.
- Test the course room, working Daf, translation drawer, and path at 320px, 768px, and desktop widths.
- Keep target sizes practical for touch and ensure text can zoom without hiding the source or Continue control.

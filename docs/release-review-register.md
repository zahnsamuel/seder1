## Promotion rule

For each encounter, a qualified reviewer records their initials and changes all six release gates to `true`: primary text, translation, context, assessment, accessibility, and safety boundary. Until then, drafts are not a substitute for legal, pastoral, or scholarly guidance.

## Review order

1. Berakhot and the pilot path.
2. The five existing deepening blocks.
3. Sanhedrin before its full interactive arc is exposed.
4. Remaining Shas labs and every capstone or repair card.

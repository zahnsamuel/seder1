## Review each source at four levels

1. **Text** — Hebrew/Aramaic exactness, reference, edition, and punctuation.
2. **Translation** — accurate, readable English; no claim stronger than the source supports.
3. **Pedagogy** — prompt has one best answer; distractors are plausible but not deceptive; explanation teaches the move.
4. **Boundary** — distinguish history, interpretation, ethical reflection, and Halakha. Do not imply personal psak.

## Repair-card review

Review the seven repair cards for question signals, source orientation, liturgical function, canonical reception, conceptual claims, ethical application, and historical context. A repair should name one move, use one short source, and return the learner to the original source rather than substitute for it.

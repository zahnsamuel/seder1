Read a dispute before trying to resolve it.

Bava Metzia begins with a shared garment and competing claims. Its core habit is intellectual fairness: keep what the source says each party holds, claims, proves, and receives clearly apart.

DISPUTE MAP

    Do not decide whose story is true before the source has told you what counts as possession, what evidence is present, and what problem the response addresses.

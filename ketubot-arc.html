A FIXED DETAIL HIDES AN INSTITUTION

Read a wedding schedule as a door into legal reasoning.

Ketubot opens with a fixed weekday, not a ruling. The Gemara's task — and yours — is to ask what real concern that schedule was built to serve, and to hold more than one real concern at once.

What to include

  • Your learner display name or profile, so your record can be found.
  • What you expected to happen and what happened instead.
  • For a content issue: the page and the specific source or question.

Data requests

You can already export or delete your own learning data yourself from your profile page — see Learner privacy for exactly what that covers. Email support if you need your sign-in identity itself removed, or if self-serve export/delete did not work as expected.

Response time

This is a small pilot, not a staffed support desk. Expect a reply within a few days, not instantly.

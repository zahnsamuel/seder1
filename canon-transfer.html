Carry a move across the canon.

Read a Torah line, its liturgical use, and a later practice question. The task is not to collapse them, but to say what changes and what persists.
Deuteronomy 6:4

שמע ישראל ה׳ אלהינו ה׳ אחד

Hear, Israel: the Lord is our God, the Lord is one.

Siddur, Shema

שמע ישראל ה׳ אלהינו ה׳ אחד

The same Torah line, encountered as recurring communal prayer.

Mishnah Berakhot 1:1

מאימתי קורין את שמע בערבית

From when do we recite Shema in the evening?

Which account best preserves the connection and difference among the three?

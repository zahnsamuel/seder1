What you can expect from Jewish Learning Academy, and what we expect of you.

Jewish Learning Academy is a study aid for Jewish learning: Gemara, Chumash, Halakha, Jewish Thought, Tefillah, Chassidus, Mussar, and Jewish History. By using it you agree to the terms below.

What Jewish Learning Academy is

A mastery-learning tool that tracks skill evidence, spaces review, and recommends what to study next. Source text, translation, and a link to the primary source (Sefaria) accompany every lesson.

What Jewish Learning Academy is not

Jewish Learning Academy does not issue halakhic rulings, provide pastoral counseling, or offer personal religious direction. Where a question depends on individual circumstances, consult a qualified rabbi or teacher directly. Do not treat any answer, explanation, or recommendation in Jewish Learning Academy as a ruling.

Your account

Keep your sign-in credentials private. You are responsible for activity under your account. You may export or delete your learning data at any time from your profile page; see Learner privacy for what that does and does not cover.

Acceptable use

Do not use Jewish Learning Academy to scrape, resell, or redistribute source content beyond personal study, attempt to bypass access controls, or submit content that is abusive or unlawful.

No warranty

Jewish Learning Academy is provided during a private pilot phase, as-is, without warranty of any kind. Content is reviewed for accuracy but may contain errors; report anything that looks wrong through support.

Changes

These terms may change as Jewish Learning Academy moves from pilot to public launch. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

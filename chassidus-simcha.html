The tradition’s strangest imperative: be joyful.

A psalm commands an emotion. The Torah’s rebuke targets service without it. The Gemara makes a particular joy the condition for the Divine Presence. And Rebbe Nachman — who knew darkness intimately — calls constant joy a great mitzvah, a lifelong discipline against despair.

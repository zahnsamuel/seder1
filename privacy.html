ס SederLEARNER PRIVACY

Your learning record is yours.

Seder uses a learner’s account details and study activity—such as placement results, attempts, XP, mastery, and review schedule—to create a private mastery path.

What we collect

Account email, display name, and the learning evidence needed to provide the service. We do not need sensitive personal information to teach a source-based lesson.

How it is protected

Authenticated learner records are separated at the database level so one learner cannot read another learner’s account or study history.

Your choices

From your profile page, you can export your full learning record as a JSON file or delete a profile and all of its learning data at any time — no support request needed. For a signed-in account, deleting your data removes your profile, mastery, attempts, reviews, and placement record, but not your sign-in identity itself (the email/login on file); to have that removed too, contact support.

Study-aid boundary

Seder supports Jewish learning. It does not provide halakhic rulings, pastoral counseling, or personal religious direction.

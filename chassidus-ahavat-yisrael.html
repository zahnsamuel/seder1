CHASSIDUS ARC · A SECOND FOUNDATION

Read love of a fellow as a Chassidic gateway.

Chassidus reads love of one’s fellow not as a nice sentiment but as a foundation the Baal Shem Tov and later masters treated as decisive. This course traces it from a Torah command, through a Tannaitic disagreement about how far it reaches, to a concrete practice.
